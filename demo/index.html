SwaraLM — Raga Retrieval Explorer

Query-by-example retrieval over Saraga Carnatic. Pick a recording; compare what different melodic representations retrieve. Green = same raga, red = different.

Similarities are cosine over precomputed embeddings (no server; everything runs in your browser). Representations: count-based baselines (bag-of-notes, transitions) vs. label-free learned embeddings (LSTM-LM, contrastive). Finding: count baselines win overall — but individual queries show where each representation succeeds and fails. Try a Kamas recording and watch for Kambhoji (same scale, different sanchara). Paper & code: github repo.
